Brickspace Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Brickspace is a Chrome extension by Orgbricks (https://orgbricks.com) for saving tab workspaces, keeping notes and opening AI chat sites in a side panel. This policy explains what the extension handles. Questions: hello@orgbricks.com.

The short version

  • Your data stays in your browser unless you turn on account sync or use an AI chat site in the panel.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What is stored on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local storage:

  • Workspaces you save: tab addresses, titles, site icons and tab groups, plus recently deleted workspaces.
  • A recent snapshot of your open tabs, refreshed every few minutes, so a session can be restored. Incognito windows are left out.
  • Notes, note templates and saved prompts. If you use "Ask AI about this page", the saved prompt contains that page's title, address and any text you selected.
  • AI chat sites you add, and the address of the conversation last open in the panel for each.
  • Your settings, schedules and launcher positions.

Site icons are loaded from the address each website provides when the extension displays them.

Pages you visit

The extension runs on web pages to show its launcher buttons and search palette. It does not collect page content or browsing activity in the background. It reads a page's title, address or selected text only when you use a feature that needs it, such as saving a tab, adding a selection to notes or asking AI about the page.

Account sync (optional)

Sync is off until you connect it in Settings. It uses the sign-in your browser already has on orgbricks.com.

  • What is sent: your notes (titles and contents) and your workspaces (names, tab addresses, titles, icons, groups and colours). Notes shared with other people are left out.
  • What the extension reads: your account email address and storage setting, and the encryption setup needed to open your data.
  • Your password or passphrase is used on your device to open your encryption key. The extension does not store or send it. The key is kept in your browser in a form that cannot be exported.
  • Data is sent over HTTPS and encrypted on your device before upload.
  • With Private storage (the default), the key is protected by your password or passphrase and Orgbricks cannot read your data. With Automatic storage, which you choose on the website, Orgbricks keeps the key, so its operators could technically decrypt your data.
  • Sync runs every few minutes while Chrome is running, and shortly after you make changes.

AI chat panel (optional)

The panel shows the AI provider's own website. What you type or upload there goes to that provider under its terms and privacy policy. Brickspace does not read or store your conversations; it remembers only the conversation's address so the panel can reopen it.

Some sites refuse to be shown inside a panel. For the AI sites in the panel's list, including ones you add, the extension removes the framing and content security policy response headers from frames that are not part of any browser tab, which is where the side panel loads them. Frames inside ordinary web pages are not changed. You can turn the AI panel off in Settings.

Backups and exports

Backup and export files are created on your device. Automatic backup saves a file to your Downloads folder and asks for download permission when you turn it on. These files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Service providers

Orgbricks runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database and storage), which processes synced data for us. See https://orgbricks.com/privacy for how Orgbricks accounts are handled.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Settings > Erase all data removes what the extension stored on this device and disconnects sync. Removing the extension also removes its local data.
  • Disconnecting sync stops transfers but keeps the copy in your account.
  • Deleting a note while connected moves it to the web app's recycle bin.
  • To delete everything in your account, use Delete my account in Orgbricks Settings, or email us.
  • Files you saved to your computer stay until you delete them.

Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's features, is not transferred except as described here or as required by law, is not used for advertising or credit decisions, and is not read by people except with your consent, for security, or to comply with the law.

Changes
